# Calzone Recipe

## Ingredients 


| Quantity | Ingredient                        |
| -------- | --------------------------------- |
| 1        | [Pizza Dough Mix](PIZZA_DOUGH.md) |
| 150g     | Warm Water                        |
| 1 tsp    | Active Dry Yeast                  |
| 1.5 tsp  | Sugar                             |
| 1 Tsp    | Olive Oil                         |
| 8 oz     | Fresh Mozzarella                  |
| Handful  | Extra Fine White Rice Flour       |

## Prep Instructions

1. Combine yeast, sugar, then water, stir, wait 5 minutes
2. Add olive oil to to yeast, water, and sugar mix, then stir
3. Add olive oil, year, water, and sugar mix to pizza dough mix, stir
4. Cover a sheet of parchment paper in white rice flour, cover hands in white
   rice flour, spread out dough mixture on the parchment paper  in a circle
   using white rice flour to keep dough from sticking
5. Place chopped mozzarella on one side of dough circle
6. Fold dough over the mozzarella, sealing into a semi-circle
6. Add vent holes using a fork or knife into the top of the calzone
7. Optionally crimp the edges of the calzone with a fork
8. Lightly coat the calzone in olive oil

## Baking Instructions - Toaster Oven

1. Cook for 15 minutes at 450 degrees F on parchment paper, remove the calzone
   and eat

## Baking Instructions - Oven

1. Place a pizza steel into the oven
2. Preheat oven to 500 degrees F
3. Use a pizza peel covered in white rice flour to cast calzone on parchment
   paper into the oven, onto the pizza steel
4. After two minutes, use the pizza steel to remove the calzone from the
   parchment paper, pull the paper out from under the calzone, and put the
   calzone back onto the pizza steel
5. After five minutes (for seven minutes total), remove the calzone and eat
